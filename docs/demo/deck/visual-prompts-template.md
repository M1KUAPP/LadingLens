# Visual prompts template

Use one card per requested visual. Replace every bracketed field only after its
content and disclosure status are confirmed. Do not add real personal data,
unverified metrics, or third-party assets without permission.

## Interface asset card

- **Purpose:** [Decision or interaction the visual explains]
- **Frame:** [Screen, viewport, or crop]
- **Visible state:** [Synthetic state and labels]
- **Primary action:** [Interaction to emphasize]
- **Evidence cue:** [Verified source or disclosure]
- **Avoid:** [Misleading implication or sensitive detail]

## Engine asset card

- **Purpose:** [System concept to explain]
- **Inputs:** [Editable input labels]
- **Transformation:** [Neutral processing description]
- **Outputs:** [Editable output labels]
- **Human boundary:** [Decision or review owner]
- **Visual treatment:** [Diagram style and hierarchy]

## Automation asset card

- **Purpose:** [Workflow transition]
- **Before:** [Manual or current-state prompt]
- **During:** [System action prompt]
- **After:** [Verified result prompt]
- **Exception:** [Failure or refusal state]
- **Disclosure:** [Automation limitation]

## Evidence asset card

- **Claim under review:** [Claim prompt]
- **Proof surface:** [Source location or artifact type]
- **Confidence:** [Verified confidence wording]
- **Interaction:** [How a viewer opens the evidence]
- **Fallback:** [Safe wording when proof is unavailable]
- **Annotation style:** [Highlight, callout, or comparison]

## State asset card

- **State name:** [Editable state]
- **Entry condition:** [Verified trigger]
- **Visible indicators:** [Labels, controls, and ownership]
- **Exit condition:** [Verified next action]
- **Error treatment:** [Visible failure wording]
- **Continuity:** [What remains visible between states]

## User asset card

- **Role:** [User role, not a personal name]
- **Context:** [Work setting]
- **Goal:** [Decision or task]
- **Constraint:** [Time, access, or policy boundary]
- **Moment:** [Interaction to depict]
- **Representation:** [Inclusive, non-identifying direction]

## Partner asset card

- **Partner type:** [Organization or system category]
- **Exchange:** [Editable input and output]
- **Boundary:** [Responsibility and approval boundary]
- **Integration state:** [Current, proposed, or illustrative]
- **Disclosure:** [Dependency or assumption]
- **Composition:** [Relationship diagram direction]

## Pilot asset card

- **Question:** [Pilot hypothesis]
- **Scope:** [Synthetic workflow and participants]
- **Success signal:** [Verified measurement prompt]
- **Review point:** [Owner and decision]
- **Stop condition:** [Safety or quality boundary]
- **Final frame:** [What the viewer should understand]
