# Alternate demo video director template

This cut favors an evidence-first reveal and a slower middle section. Keep all
content synthetic and distinguish live material from a visibly labelled,
verbally disclosed prepared fallback.

## Narrative pacing

- **Cold open:** [Begin on a source artifact or unresolved state.]
- **Context compression:** [Explain the workflow in one verified sentence.]
- **Reveal:** [Move from evidence to the product interface.]
- **Slow inspection:** [Pause on provenance, ownership, and limitations.]
- **Contrast:** [Show a safe refusal or alternate state.]
- **Resolution:** [Return to the initial artifact with a verified next action.]
- **Close:** [State the approved takeaway and invitation.]

## Director blocks

### Evidence-first open

- **TIME:** [Start and end time]
- **ON SCREEN:** [Synthetic source and inspectable evidence]
- **VOICEOVER:** [Question the evidence will answer]
- **PACE:** [Short opening, then hold]
- **EDITOR NOTE:** [Avoid revealing the conclusion before the source]

### Deliberate inspection

- **TIME:** [Start and end time]
- **ON SCREEN:** [Source, system state, and owner together]
- **VOICEOVER:** [Verified explanation and limitation]
- **PACE:** [Slow enough for unaided reading]
- **EDITOR NOTE:** [Use continuity cuts; keep labels stable]

### Resolution and close

- **TIME:** [Start and end time]
- **ON SCREEN:** [Resolved state, remaining exception, and next action]
- **VOICEOVER:** [Safe summary and invitation]
- **PACE:** [Accelerate briefly, then settle on the end frame]
- **EDITOR NOTE:** [Retain the disclosure label through the cut]

## Shot checklist

- [ ] [Synthetic inputs are visibly identified.]
- [ ] [Live and prepared modes cannot be confused.]
- [ ] [Every claim has a proof surface or safe fallback wording.]
- [ ] [Cursor movement and focus order are rehearsed.]
- [ ] [Text remains readable at delivery resolution.]
- [ ] [Failure, refusal, and ownership states remain visible long enough.]
- [ ] [No personal, confidential, or licensed material appears.]
- [ ] [Final frame contains the verified next action.]
