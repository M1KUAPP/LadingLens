## 17:28 Problem statement: shipping-document verification

**[00:17:28] Emcee:** Thank you, Ms Chin. Now let's get into the main challenge. Let us welcome **Mr Sergio, software
engineer from Averis**, to announce the problem statement.

**[00:18:03] Sergio:** The challenge you'll solve during this hackathon is **shipping-document verification**. At
Averis, we have a shipping operations team that receives many kinds of message in the same email inbox: requests to
prepare documents, shipping instructions, invoice questions and, like any inbox, spam.

**[00:18:44] Sergio:** For document-checking requests specifically, the team compares the **shipping instruction**,
which came as an attachment in an earlier email, against the **draft bill of lading**, a document prepared for
the shipping company. If the bill of lading doesn't match the shipping instruction, they have to rectify it, adjusting
until both match. **The goal is to catch incorrect details in the bill of lading before the draft is finalised.**

**[00:19:47] Sergio:** Common problems:

1. **Finding the right email takes time.** In real life, the shipping docs team can receive **up to 2,000 emails a
   day**.
2. **Manual comparison is repetitive and easy to get wrong.** For each email they have to read it, understand the
   instruction, open the attachment, and check it against the reference documents.
3. **Terminology differs.** The bill of lading is handled by the shipping company, so the terms used on the shipping
   instruction and the bill of lading don't always match.

**[00:21:01] Sergio:** So what we want the system to do:

- **Classify the email**: is it about invoices, is it spam, is it a shipping instruction, a draft bill of lading, a
  bill-of-lading confirmation?
- **Extract the information**, whether it's in the email body or in an attachment.
- **Compare**: once it finds an attachment containing a draft bill of lading, compare it against the existing shipping
  instruction.
- **Ask for help**: when the system can't classify, extract or compare correctly, it should always ask the shipping
  docs team for clarification.

**[00:22:26] Sergio:** The organisers will share sample data we've prepared. There are two parts. One you can use in
your application as-is: **around 500 emails and hundreds of attachments**. The other, if you're familiar with Docker,
provides an API for you. You can design the system however you like, with any technology you think is suitable.

> **Official** _[Bundle]_: the static bundle holds **520 emails** as JSON in `inbox/` and **250 attachments**:
> 192 `.txt`, 28 `.pdf`, 22 `.xlsx` and 8 `.docx`. It also has `sample_submission.json` and `loader.py`. The Docker
> bundle serves the same data at `http://localhost:8080` (`docker compose up --build`). `loader.py` works the same
> way for both: `Inbox("data")` or `Inbox("http://localhost:8080")` _[PS p.3]_.

**[00:23:20] Sergio:** The sample data is already simplified. The common fields to extract are:

- Shipper
- Consignee
- Notify party
- Port of loading (where the shipment starts)
- Port of discharge (where the shipment ends)
- Container count
- Gross weight in kilograms

> **Official** _[PS p.1–2]_, _[Bundle]_: the official document is titled _"Shipping document verification: from
> email inbox to discrepancy report"_.
>
> - **Five email categories.** Each email is classified as `BL_COMPARISON`, `SI_REQUEST`, `INVOICE_QUERY`, `GENERAL`
>   or `SPAM`. Only document-comparison requests go on to the checking step.
> - **The seven fields** have these submission keys: `shipper`, `consignee`, `notify_party`, `port_of_loading`,
>   `port_of_discharge`, `container_count`, `gross_weight_kg`.
> - **The SI is the reference.** When a field differs, show both values side by side (e.g. _SI: 3 / BL: 4_). If all
>   seven match, report _"No mismatch detected."_
> - **Status per email** is `OK`, `MISMATCH` or `NEEDS_REVIEW`. A `NEEDS_REVIEW` result carries a `review_reason`:
>   `wrong_doc_type`, `missing_attachment`, `unreadable` or `missing_value`. This is the "ask for help" behaviour
>   Sergio described.
> - **Different labels, same field.** The SI and BL name fields differently: "Port of Loading" vs "Load Port" vs
>   "POL", "Discharge Port" vs "POD". _[Docker]_ lists every label variant.
> - **Advanced stage** _[PS p.2]_: PDF and Word attachments, scanned documents (OCR or a vision-capable LLM),
>   messier inputs such as misleading subjects and missing attachments, and a human review flow with retries. Emcee:\*\* You can scan the QR code for the problem statement. The Google Drive link there also has the zip
>   files of the sample data Sergio mentioned.
