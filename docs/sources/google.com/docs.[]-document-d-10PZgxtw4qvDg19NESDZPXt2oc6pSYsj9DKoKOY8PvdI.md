## Eligibility

- Teams must consist of students from digital technology related backgrounds
  (IT, CS, Software Engineering, etc.). Both undergraduate and postgraduate
  students from any university are eligible, including double-degree students
  where at least one degree is in IT or Computer Science related fields.
- Participation is open only to Malaysian citizens and foreign nationals
  currently studying at a university or educational institution in Malaysia.
- Judges, mentors, sponsors, and organizers are not eligible to participate.

## Participation guidelines

- Teams must consist of 2 to 5 members only.
- Switching members is prohibited unless approved by organizers. Contact Ming
  Dong (+60123688837) for approval requests.
- Once registered, participants can sign into the participant portal at
  [https://averisxmonashhackathon2026.my](https://averisxmonashhackathon2026.my).
- Once the registration deadline has passed, team changes will no longer be
  allowed.
- Participants may form teams with members from different institutions.
- Each participant may only be a member of one team. If a participant is found
  registered or participating in more than one team, both teams will be
  disqualified.
- Registration for the Averis x Monash Hackathon 2026 is free of charge (FOC).
- The official communication platform used throughout the event is Discord.
- Any tampering with the competition registration, workflow, or results will
  result in immediate disqualification.
- Any form of harassment, discrimination, or disruptive behavior towards
  participants, mentors, judges, or organizers will cause immediate
  disqualification.

## Submission guidelines

- The submitted solution must incorporate Artificial Intelligence (AI)
  technology as a key component.
- All work on the project must be completed during the official duration of the
  hackathon.
- The submission and all its components must be the team's original work. While
  referencing existing solutions is permitted, direct plagiarism of ideas,
  code, or designs is strictly prohibited.
- Participants are not allowed to plagiarize the ideas, work, or submissions of
  other teams.
- All submissions must be made before the specified deadline. Incomplete,
  illegible, misdirected, or late submissions will not be considered.
- Submissions containing dangerous, vulgar, offensive, indecent, illegal,
  racist, plagiarized, cruel, or fraudulent content—or infringing upon the
  rights or copyright of others—will cause immediate disqualification.

### Submission criteria

Preliminary round requirements:

- At a minimum, your submission should be a low-code solution.
- A semi-working prototype is strongly encouraged.
- No-code submissions will not be accepted.
- Slide deck, video demo, and prototype link must be submitted.

Final round requirements:

- A working prototype is expected from participants.
- The final submission should be an extension and improvement of your
  preliminary round entry.

### AI usage requirement

- All submissions must incorporate AI and utilize cloud infrastructure as part
  of the solution's development, deployment, or core functionality.
- Solutions that do not meaningfully integrate cloud infrastructure may receive
  significantly reduced scores.

### Technical stack

- Teams are free to use any programming languages, frameworks, platforms,
  libraries, APIs, or development tools of their choice. There are no
  restrictions on the technical stack used.
- Teams are encouraged to adopt a well-designed software architecture that
  supports the functionality and objectives of their project.
- Consider both short-term implementation and long-term potential, including
  scalability, maintainability, extensibility, and future development beyond the
  hackathon.

## Awards

- All awards and prizes must be shared among all team members per mutual
  agreement.
- Prizes are non-transferable and can only be claimed by designated winners.
- The decisions of the judges and organizers regarding winner selection are
  final and binding. No appeals, disputes, or reconsiderations will be
  entertained.

## Data protection and privacy

- Personal information shared with the hackathon will be kept secure and used
  only in line with these rules and regulations unless specific consent is
  granted.

- Participants consent to submitting resumes, which will be shared with event

  Sponsors receiving resumes are expected to handle information responsibly and

## Judging criteria

### Evaluation criteria distribution

Submissions are evaluated across seven core dimensions:

- Working Core Prototype: 25%
- System Design & Architecture: 15%
- Technology Integration (TBC): 15%
- Technical Feasibility & Validation: 15%
- Problem Statement Understanding: 10%
- Innovation & Solution Approach: 10%
- Practical Value & Potential: 10%

## Submission procedure

### Submission details

- **Platform**: Google Forms
- **Submission link**:
  [Official Submission Form](https://forms.gle/nnam5eXrf5cjXdf3)
- **Submission period**: 18 September 2026 – 22 September 2026 (12:00 PM MYT)

### Mandatory submission components

1.  **Project description**: Brief summary of the project, including its name,
    purpose, and problem statement addressed.
2.  **Demo video link**:
    - Maximum duration: 5 minutes (for every 30-second delay beyond 5 minutes, 1
      mark is deducted).
    - YouTube video setting: unlisted or public (private videos cannot be
      viewed). Google Drive video links must be set to "Anyone with the link ->
      Viewer".
    - Video must cover: quick intro (team & project name), the problem, tech
      stack, live demo walkthrough of working prototype, and impact/results.
3.  **GitHub repository link**: Public link to project source code with a clear
    `README.md` file containing setup and execution instructions.
4.  **Live prototype / demo link**: Publicly accessible link to the deployed
    project, functional throughout the judging period.
5.  **Slide deck / documentation link**: Public link to slides or documentation
    (Google Slides, PDF on Google Drive, Notion, or GitHub) covering technical
    architecture, implementation details, challenges faced, and future roadmap.

### Google Forms submission structure

#### Part 1: Team details

1.  Team name
2.  Team representative details (name, email, contact number)

#### Part 2: Project details

1.  Project name
2.  Project description and summary
3.  GitHub repository link
4.  Live prototype / demo link
5.  Slide deck / documentation link
6.  Video demo link (maximum 5 minutes)
