# Averis x Monash Hackathon 2026

The Averis x Monash Hackathon 2026 brings together student developers to solve
a real-world shipping operations challenge in four days. Submissions close on
22 September 2026 at 12:00 PM Malaysian time (MYT, UTC+8).

## Timeline

### Registration opens

The participant portal opens and teams can register. Form a team of 2 to 5
members and submit your entry.

- Date: 1 September 2026

### Registration closes

No new team registrations are accepted after this point. Confirm every member of
your team before this date.

- Date: 18 September 2026

### Opening ceremony

The problem statement is released, along with the timeline and submission
requirements. Attend if you are competing.

- Date: 18 September 2026 (Virtual)

### Build period

Four days to build, with two workshops along the way.

- Date: 18 September – 22 September 2026

### Submission deadline

Send your repository link, slide deck, video demo, and demo URL through the
submission form. The form accepts updates right up to the deadline.

- Date: 22 September 2026, 12:00 PM MYT

### Judging period

Judges score every submission against the published criteria. Scores are
weighted and averaged across judges.

- Date: 22 September – 23 September 2026

### Results announced

Preliminary evaluation scores, rubric breakdowns, and certified PDF reports are
released. Top 10 finalist qualifications are announced for the Grand Finals.

- Date: 23 September 2026

### Final pitch day

Top 10 finalist squads deliver in-person 10-minute pitches with 5-minute Q&A,
judged live on paper score sheets for the RM 9,000 prize pool.

- Date: 26 September 2026 (Monash University Malaysia)

## Theme and problem statement

Official challenge brief and requirements for the hackathon.

### Shipping document verification

From email inbox to discrepancy report:

Averis receives high volumes of shipping operational emails requiring staff to
compare Shipping Instructions (SI) against draft Bills of Lading (BL).
Participants must build an automated system to classify emails, extract shipment
fields, detect mismatches across seven key attributes (shipper, consignee,
notify party, ports, container count, and gross weight), and escalate complex
edge cases to human reviewers.

Key system capabilities:

- Email classification
- Document field extraction
- Seven-field discrepancy detection
- Human-in-the-loop escalation

Download the official brief:
[Download Problem Statement (PDF)](https://averisxmonashhackathon2026.my/documents/problem-statement.pdf).

### Scoring rubric breakdown

Submissions are evaluated out of 100 total points across seven core dimensions:

- Working Core Prototype: 25% of the final score
- System Design & Architecture: 15% of the final score
- Technology Integration (TBC): 15% of the final score
- Technical Feasibility & Validation: 15% of the final score
- Problem Statement Understanding: 10% of the final score
- Innovation & Solution Approach: 10% of the final score
- Practical Value & Potential: 10% of the final score

## Frequently asked questions

### Who can participate?

This hackathon only accepts Malaysian and international students currently
enrolled in a Malaysian university or college. You do not have to be at Monash
University Malaysia. Please register with your personal email address. Teams
consist of 2 to 5 members.

### Do I need to know how to code?

Not everyone on the team does. Designers, product specialists, and domain
experts are all valuable. However, every project needs a working prototype or
demo, so at least one person must be comfortable building.

### What should I submit?

A GitHub repository containing your project source code, a video demo
walkthrough (5 minutes maximum), a slide deck presentation for your solution,
and a live deployment or prototype URL if available. All deliverables are
submitted via the official submission form provided to registered teams.

### How are submissions judged?

In two stages. Judges from Averis score every eligible submission against System
Design & Architecture (15%), Working Core Prototype (25%), Technology
Integration (TBC) (15%), Technical Feasibility & Validation (15%), Problem
Statement Understanding (10%), Innovation & Solution Approach (10%), and
Practical Value & Potential (10%).

Judges score over two days following the deadline, and the ten highest-scoring
teams advance as finalists to pitch live on Final Pitch Day.

### Are there prizes?

Three cash prizes are awarded at the end of Final Pitch Day:

- 1st Place: RM 5,000
- 2nd Place: RM 3,000
- 3rd Place: RM 1,000

Every finalist team pitches to the Averis panel regardless of final placement.
