## Registration opens

The participant portal opens and teams can register. Gather a team of 2 to 5
members and submit your entry.

- Date: 1 September 2026

## Registration closes

No new team registrations are accepted after this point. Confirm every member
of your team before this deadline.

- Date: 18 September 2026

## Opening ceremony

The problem statement is officially released along with the timeline and
submission requirements.

- Date: 18 September 2026 (Virtual)

## Build period

Four days to build and develop your solution, featuring two workshops along
the way.

- Date: 18 September – 22 September 2026

## Submission deadline

Send your repository link, slide deck, video demo, and demo URL through the
official submission form. The form accepts updates right up to the deadline.

- Date: 22 September 2026, 12:00 PM MYT

## Judging period

Judges score every submission against the published criteria: system design and
architecture, working core prototype, technology integration, technical
feasibility and validation, problem statement understanding, innovation, and
practical value. Scores are weighted and averaged across judges.

- Date: 22 September – 23 September 2026

## Results announced

Preliminary evaluation scores, rubric breakdowns, and certified PDF reports are
released. Top 10 finalist qualifications are announced for the Grand Finals.

- Date: 23 September 2026

## Final pitch day

Top 10 finalist squads deliver in-person 10-minute pitches with 5-minute Q&A,
judged live on paper score sheets for the RM 9,000 prize pool.

- Date: 26 September 2026 (Monash University Malaysia)
