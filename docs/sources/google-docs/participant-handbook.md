## Introduction

Welcome to Averis x Monash Hackathon 2026!

This hackathon brings together creative minds, tech enthusiasts, and
changemakers to tackle a real-world shipping operations challenge through
intelligent automation. Whether you are just starting out or are a seasoned
developer, this is your chance to explore AI, build practical solutions, and
bring your ideas to life.

This information pack contains everything you need to know, from competition
rules and evaluation criteria to project submission instructions. Make sure to
go through it thoroughly so you are well prepared for your hackathon journey.

## Prizes and awards

- All participants will receive a LinkedIn badge and e-certificate of
  participation, provided they participated under a registered team and
  submitted their project.
- Finalists will receive a separate finalist e-certificate recognizing their
  achievement in reaching the Final Round.
- Cash prizes will be awarded to the top three finalist teams after all rounds
  conclude:
  - 1st Place: RM 5,000
  - 2nd Place: RM 3,000
  - 3rd Place: RM 1,000

## Conclusion

Averis x Monash Hackathon 2026 is a venue to experiment, collaborate, and build
technology that solves a genuine industry operational challenge. From
classifying incoming emails to extracting shipping information and detecting
critical discrepancies, turn real-world data into an impactful solution.
